CANDIDATE RESEARCH / PERSONAL TASTE

Good taste.
But whose taste?

Could AI learn what one person prefers from a small number of explicit choices? We could study personalization that respects the purpose of a choice, rather than assuming one design is best for everyone.

New research proposal · illustrative preferences
THE PROBLEM / POPULAR IS NOT PERSONAL

The same room.
Two different
feelings.

Someone may prefer a quiet room for relaxing and a brighter one for entertaining. A preference is a choice made by a person, in a context.

Why this would matter

Visual search, shopping, and generative design tools that better reflect an individual’s choices. AR could let people compare options in the space they actually care about.

THE DATA / CHOICES WITH CONTEXT

A few choices.
A new test.
Less guessing.

Collect comparisons, the stated purpose, and optional reasons. Include “neither” and uncertain choices. Test whether the model predicts a later choice that it has not seen.

The research output

A permissioned dataset of visual preferences across people and situations, plus a benchmark for learning from a small personal history.

A FIRST SMALL STUDY

Can a few choices
say more than “modern”?

Begin with one domain, such as room styles. Compare a few example selections with a short written style preference and the overall most popular choice.

01

Ask for a real choice.

Present paired alternatives with a specific purpose. Record explicit preference, uncertainty, and optional reasons. Looking is not a preference label.

02

Keep a future choice unseen.

Measure personal prediction accuracy on held-out choices. Give every method the same number of examples; evaluate new participants separately.

03

Change the context.

Repeat later or with a different purpose. Check whether the model can adapt instead of locking the person into a fixed style.

EXISTING WORK TO BUILD ON

PARA already studies personalized image aesthetics. Our proposed emphasis is context-dependent choices in a concrete design task. Whether this adds useful training data or beats existing personalization methods is something to test.
