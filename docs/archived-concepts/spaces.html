CANDIDATE RESEARCH / SPATIAL UNDERSTANDING

It looks right.
Does it actually fit?

A room is a place to move through, open things, and live in. We could study whether AI understands those constraints, using measured spaces and AR to check its predictions.

New research proposal · schematic examples
THE PROBLEM / APPEARANCE IS NOT FEASIBILITY

There’s space
for the sofa.
But can you get past?

A model might suggest a convincing arrangement while blocking a doorway. The useful question is whether a specific object, placement, and route work together.

Why this would matter

More reliable room planning, shopping previews, and AR placement tools. A model should explain the blocking constraint or ask for a missing measurement.

THE DATA / MEASURED CONSTRAINTS

Give the model
something it can
check.

Pair a room scan with object dimensions, a placement request, and checked outcomes. Include near misses, awkward door swings, and incomplete scans.

The research output

A dataset of spatial questions with measurable answers. It could support multimodal spatial models and independent tests of room-planning systems.

A FIRST SMALL STUDY

One room.
Several near misses.

Begin with measured floor plans and a small collection of furniture footprints. A footprint study tests 2D placement; it does not establish that a real object can be carried through a doorway.

01

Define the constraints.

Set explicit clearance and door-swing requirements. Record dimensions and uncertainty instead of judging a layout by appearance.

02

Compare ways of reasoning.

Compare a vision-language model with a geometry checker and a model that can call that checker. Keep the available measurements matched.

03

Test new spaces.

Measure valid placements, missed collisions, and justified uncertainty on unseen rooms and object combinations.

EXISTING WORK TO BUILD ON

ScanQA and SQA3D already study questions about 3D scenes. Our proposed emphasis is measurable placement constraints and incomplete observations. We would check novelty and demonstrate what real capture adds over synthetic layouts.
