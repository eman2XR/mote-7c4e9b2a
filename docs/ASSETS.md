# Asset provenance

## Morning scene

- Final asset: `site/assets/morning-scene.jpg`.
- Created with the built-in image-generation tool; no API-key CLI or paid API workflow was used.
- Generated image was encoded as JPEG at quality 85 for the website; scene content was not otherwise edited.
- This is illustrative imagery, not captured participant data. The UI and exported example records identify it as such.

### Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: photographic scene for an interactive website concept about personal visual memory, no UI. Primary request: a beautifully art-directed yet believable first-person view of an ordinary morning at a small light-oak kitchen table in a warm modern home. Landscape 3:2 composition, 1536x1024. Camera looking down at approximately 50 degrees, enough context to recognize the room. On the left half of the table is a closed cream notebook with subtle texture and a dark green pen. A ceramic burnt-orange coffee mug is above the notebook, near the center-left. A pair of silver house keys with a clearly visible muted green leather key fob lies separately at center-right on the table. On the far right is an open cream canvas tote bag, with a coiled white USB cable and small white charger clearly visible near its opening. A little potted plant and a sunlit window are in the softly blurred background. Precise objects must be separate and easy to distinguish; keys must be clearly recognizable and the bag opening should be visible. Soft late-morning sunlight casts long diagonal window shadows across the honey-colored wood, warm cream, olive, terracotta, natural film texture, sophisticated independent lifestyle magazine photography. Lived-in and calm, realistic object proportions, premium photography, not a 3D rendering. No people or hands. No text, branding, watermarks, UI, icons, borders, or collage. The whole image is one coherent photograph. Keep the main objects within the middle 80% of the frame.
